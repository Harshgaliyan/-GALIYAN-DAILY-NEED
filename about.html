How to use this website

1. Browse products

Start from Home, Categories, or Products. Use category and brand filters to find the right item quickly.

2. View product details

Click a popular product to open its image, price, product information, and enquiry form.

3. Add to cart

No account is needed. Add items, then enter your name, phone number, and delivery address. Email is optional.

4. Request your order

Review your cart and send the order request through WhatsApp. The store must confirm stock, delivery coverage, and final amount before your order is accepted.

Privacy policy

Your cart and saved delivery address are stored in this browser. When you request an order, the site prepares a WhatsApp message; your details are shared with the store only if you choose to send that message.

  • Do not enter sensitive payment passwords.
  • You can clear your saved address and cart by clearing this site's browser storage.
  • Contact us if you need help with your information.
